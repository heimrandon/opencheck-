TRACK 2 — Clinical expert for AI (your MMPI/PAI + assessment background is the edge)

What these are: AI labs pay psychology/mental-health experts to evaluate, red-team, and annotate how AI models talk about mental health — testing them on CBT/DBT, assessment tools, psychometrics, diagnostic reasoning, and safety. Your master's + psychometrist experience is exactly the profile they pay premium rates for.

Expert platforms — apply once, get matched to projects [PORTAL]
• Mercor — Psychology Expert ⭐ — author/review/annotate psychology tasks that evaluate AI reasoning about human behavior. Advanced degree valued (you qualify well). Highest-paying of the group. → work.mercor.com/explore (expect an AI interview + assessments; 2–4 wks to hear back).
• Outlier (Scale AI) — AI Training for Psychology ⭐ — train models to be accurate/safe in psychology. → app.outlier.ai/en/expert/opportunities (sign up → ID verify → skill assessment → matched projects, weekly pay).
• DataAnnotation — paid AI training incl. psychology tasks. → datannotation.tech
• Handshake AI — connects grads/professionals with AI labs for training roles. → joinhandshake.com (Handshake AI).

Honest framing: these are contractor / flexible-hours gigs ($30–$75+/hr depending on expertise), NOT salaried jobs with benefits. Best used two ways: (1) supplemental income while you land a full-time ops role, and (2) a résumé differentiator — "evaluated LLM outputs on clinical/psychometric accuracy and safety" is a standout line that bridges your clinical past and an ops/AI future.
